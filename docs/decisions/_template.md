# NNNN. Short title in the imperative

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by NNNN (link it)
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What forced a decision. The constraints, the pressure, what was true at the
time. Someone reading this in two years has none of your context — give it to
them.

## Decision

What was chosen, stated plainly.

## Alternatives considered

What else was on the table and why it lost. This is the part that stops the
question being reopened from scratch.

## Consequences

What this makes easy, what it makes hard, and what it costs. Be honest about the
downside — a record that lists only benefits is advocacy, not a decision record.
